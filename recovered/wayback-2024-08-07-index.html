Welcome to the temporary home of Bountiful Mental Health.

Bountiful Mental Health offers exceptional psychiatric services for children, adolescents, adults, and families to optimize quality of life for our clients.

Treatment starts with a thorough evaluation to identify the correct psychiatric diagnosis as well as biological, environmental, social, or other factors which may contribute to it. Labs are often ordered to assist in diagnosis and treatment monitoring. The evaluation results are used to create a personalized treatment plan with the patient's values and preferences in mind.

Our practice is small, and clients are seen as frequently as needed to treat their conditions; this allows for timely treatment adjustments and faster return to health.

Care is coordinated with other treatment providers (PCP's, pediatricians, therapists) to ensure the best care.
