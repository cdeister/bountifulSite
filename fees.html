Insurance

Out-of-network reimbursement

Bountiful Mental Health is an out-of-network practice. Many health insurance plans reimburse 50–80% of session fees with out-of-network providers. We provide superbills, and claims can be submitted through the Reimbursify service — by you or by us — whichever is easier.

When checking your out-of-network benefits with your insurance carrier, it helps to ask about your deductible and the “allowed amount” for the codes that will be billed:

  • 90792 — Initial psychiatric evaluation
  • 99213 / 99214 — Medical/psychiatric visit (by complexity)
  • 90833 — Therapy add-on, 30-min visit
  • 90836 — Therapy add-on, 60-min visit

Lab tests ordered as part of nutritional treatment are separate from session fees and are often covered by insurance.
