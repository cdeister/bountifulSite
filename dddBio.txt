Dr. Deister received her Bachelor of Science in Chemistry from the California Institute of Technology (Caltech) and Master of Science in Biology at the University of Houston.  She uses her extensive scientific background to inform her medical treatment of patients at the biochemical level. Dr. Deister received her MD from the Long School of Medicine at UT Health San Antonio, where she also attended psychiatry residency and was the Assistant Chief Resident.  She attended the child and adolescent psychiatry fellowship and was Co-chief Resident at Brown University, Alpert Medical School/Bradley Hospital.  Previously, she taught and practiced at Boston Children’s Hospital/Harvard Medical School in the Developmental Medicine Center, the Division of Addiction Medicine, and the Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences.
Dr. Deister treats all ages and all disorders, with notable experience in autism, Down Syndrome, catatonia, OCD, and addictions.  She is trained in revolutionary nutritional therapies for treating psychiatric disorders, state-of-the-art psychopharmacology including use of clozapine, and evidence-based therapy approaches for individuals and families.  She has treated active-duty military service members and veterans. Her awards include the Senior Medical Student in Psychiatry Award and the Senior Resident Award for Clinical Excellence in Psychiatry at the Long School of Medicine, and twice the Goldman Teaching Award in Psychiatry at Boston Children’s Hospital/Harvard Medical School in 2018 and 2023.  Dr. Deister has presented at national conferences for the American Psychiatric Association (APA), American Academy of Child and Adolescent Psychiatry (AACAP) and the American Association of Addiction Psychiatry (AAAP).  She is a member of AACAP and the AACAP Family Committee.
