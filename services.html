Core services

What we offer

Psychiatric Evaluation

A comprehensive 120-minute initial evaluation covering history, symptoms, and the biological, psychological, and environmental factors behind them. Children and parents are seen separately for parts of the session when possible.

Medication Management

Careful, evidence-based medication treatment with frequent follow-up — visits happen as often as needed so adjustments are timely and recovery is faster.

Therapy

Motivational interviewing, exposure and response prevention (ERP) for OCD, CBT, family therapy, mindfulness practices, and strength-based approaches — often in the same visit as medical care.

Nutritional & Metabolic Treatment

Evaluation and treatment with nutrients alongside or instead of medications, guided by lab testing and Walsh Research Institute protocols. Metabolic treatment with ketogenic and other therapeutic diets.

What to expect

Your first visit

1. Free consultation. A short introductory call to meet Dr. Deister, ask questions, and understand her approach before you begin.

2. Initial evaluation (120 minutes). A deep review of your history and concerns, ending with an initial treatment plan built around your values and preferences.

3. Labs, if helpful. For clients interested in nutritional approaches, lab tests target treatment precisely — and are often covered by insurance.

4. Ongoing care. Weekly, every-other-week, or monthly visits depending on need, with care coordinated across your PCP, pediatrician, or therapist.
