About Dr. Diana Deister

Child, adolescent, adult, and family psychiatrist — and founder of Bountiful Mental Health.

In her words

A scientist’s curiosity, a clinician’s care

“I enjoy using my strong science background to create a developmental understanding of the factors which cause mental health problems for my clients. Then we work together to address them using therapy, nutrition, medications, or other treatments as needed.

My favorite therapy approaches include motivational interviewing to increase motivation for behavioral change, exposure and response prevention (ERP) for OCD, and family therapy. I have experience using various dietary approaches for mental health, including elimination diets for food intolerances, gluten-free, dairy-free, low-carb, ketogenic, and carnivore diets.”

Approach

Comfort, thoroughness, and a one-stop shop

Dr. Deister strives to provide a comforting atmosphere in which patients can discuss their problems fully. She addresses the biological, psychological, and environmental aspects of mental health to provide a “one-stop shop” for busy professionals, families, and students — an integrated approach that acknowledges the real connections between body, mind, family, and communities.

Her initial evaluation is 120 minutes, allowing adequate time to assess the problem and discuss the initial treatment approach. When possible, children and parents are seen separately for parts of the session; for adult patients, family involvement is welcomed.
